Gain deeper historical context with interactive timelines and primary sources across politics, culture, and society

Lightweight, fast, and built for discovery. Our platform brings together archival footage, personal testimonies, interactive maps, declassified records, and data visualizations — all in one place.

  • [01]

    Data-Driven Historical Context

    ChronoUkraine connects fragmented historical records into a unified, searchable timeline. By pairing archival documents with demographic and economic data, the platform reveals the broader patterns behind Ukraine's transformation over the past three decades.

  • [02]

    Continuous Archival Updates

    ChronoUkraine evolves alongside ongoing historical research. We continuously integrate newly digitized archives, oral histories, and contemporary documentation, ensuring the platform remains a living, growing repository of Ukraine’s modern story.

  • [03]

    Connecting Eras & Perspectives

    History isn't static—it's a continuous thread. Our modular timeline links key events across decades, showing how the civic movements of the 1990s shaped the democratic breakthroughs of 2004, 2014, and today. Every entry is cross-referenced with verified sources and multimedia artifacts.

  • [04]

    Open Platform for Researchers & Storytellers

    For educators, journalists, and independent historians, ChronoUkraine provides an open framework to share verified narratives. Our tools make it easy to curate, contextualize, and present primary sources—from classroom lessons to public exhibitions.
